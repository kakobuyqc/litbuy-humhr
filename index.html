The quick answer

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list associated with shopping through Litbuy. Entries may organize product links, categories, prices and notes in a table. Its usefulness depends on the source and freshness of those entries: a spreadsheet alone does not establish availability, authenticity, seller reliability or final delivered cost.

03 / The Litbuy spreadsheet guide

Turn a long list into a useful shortlist.

A practical Litbuy shopping guide starts with a repeatable process. These four steps help you compare product links without losing the details behind each choice.

  1. 01

    Choose one category and a clear brief.

    Start with sneaker finds, everyday T-shirts, outerwear or accessories. Define the style, size and budget before collecting dozens of unrelated links.

  2. 02

    Record the exact listing and variant.

    Save the product URL, seller name, model, color and selected size. Separate confirmed listing details from assumptions or comments copied from another shopping list.

  3. 03

    Compare evidence and total cost.

    Review available quality-check photos, size measurements and material descriptions. Keep the item price separate from shipping estimates, service fees and any applicable import charges.

  4. 04

    Reopen the source before deciding.

    Check that the link still resolves to the intended item. Reconfirm stock status, current pricing, return conditions and the checkout terms of the service you actually use.

04 / The comparison framework

What belongs in a product spreadsheet?

Use this structure for Litbuy product links, a fashion spreadsheet or a personal shopping tracker. It works without assuming every listing contains the same information.

Suggested research fields — examples of information to collect, not live product data.
Field What to record Why it helps
Product identity Product name, original link, seller and exact variant Distinguishes similar items and prevents link mix-ups.
Fit and dimensions Size chart, garment measurements, shoe length or accessory dimensions Makes size comparison more meaningful than a label alone.
Materials and construction Declared composition, lining, closures and visible details Helps compare intended use and identify unanswered questions.
Photos and evidence Listing images, available QC photos and their source Shows which observations relate to the item being considered.
Price and currency Item price, currency, variant and date checked Prevents outdated or differently priced variants from distorting comparisons.
Delivery budget Available shipping quote, package estimate and separate charges Distinguishes the listing price from an estimated landed cost.
Decision status Researching, shortlisted, needs clarification or removed Keeps the product list manageable as information changes.

Read a low price in context.

A budget find is only comparable when the size, variant and included costs match. Keep currency conversion notes and delivery estimates separate, and mark unknown costs as unknown instead of treating them as zero.

Keep a useful research trail.

Add a “last checked” field to your spreadsheet template. Product availability, seller details and item descriptions can change; a saved link or screenshot is a research reference rather than a guarantee.

05 / Clear answers

Before you follow another product link.

Short answers to common questions about spreadsheet finds, product comparison and using this page.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent informational page. It is not operated by Litbuy and does not claim a partnership with Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. Navigation and category links lead to CAFANS.

Where can I browse the products linked from this guide?

Use the category buttons near the top of the page to browse CAFANS. The Shoes, Hoodies/Sweaters, T-Shirts, Jackets and other buttons open their respective CAFANS categories. This page does not confirm whether an individual item is available through Litbuy.

Can I download a Litbuy spreadsheet here?

No downloadable spreadsheet is provided on this page. The comparison table is a suggested structure you can copy into your own spreadsheet, with columns for links, sizes, prices, evidence and research status.

How do I check whether a spreadsheet entry is still useful?

Open the original listing and compare its title, images, seller, variant and current price with the saved entry. Check availability and the date of any supporting photos. If the source is missing or has changed, mark the entry for review.

Do QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. Quality-check photos can help you inspect visible details, but they do not establish authenticity, ownership of trademarks or seller authorization. For branded purchases, verify provenance and use appropriate authentication evidence.

Is the product price the final amount I will pay?

Not necessarily. Depending on the purchase and destination, the total may include delivery, service fees, currency conversion and applicable import charges. Confirm the actual charges and terms at checkout.

What is the best way to compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check how the seller measures chest width, length, waist and inseam, and account for the stated fit and fabric stretch.
